Our Services & Programs


Youth Peer Support Groups

Weekly facilitated discussions for young adults seeking a safe space to share experiences and build coping strategies with trained peer leaders.

Addiction Recovery & Counselling

Navigating substance abuse with confidential guidance, educational materials, and structured referral pathways to accredited treatment centers.

Educational Workshops & Outreach

Interactive mental health awareness workshops covering stress management, depression, and anxiety identification for schools and university campuses.

Family Support & Crisis Resources

Specialized guidance for family members and guardians, equipping them with tools to navigate a loved one's crisis and manage grief.

Youth Tele-Counselling Line

Free, confidential phone and chat consultations with professional counsellors for youth experiencing sudden panic, social anxiety, or exam stress.

High School Mental Wellness Drives

On-site campus campaigns educating high school learners on cyberbullying prevention, exam stress management, and early depression signs.

Trauma & Grief Support Groups

Structured support circles tailored for young adults grieving sudden loss, traumatic events, or family bereavement in safe group settings.

Substance Rehabilitation Aftercare

Long-term relapse prevention circles and sober peer mentorship guiding recovering individuals as they transition back into university and work.
